# Surface brief: VELORA ESTATES site (all routes)

Scope: whole site — Home (Persuade), Properties / Property / Favorites (Operate), Locations, Agents, About, Contact. Visitor mode for Home: Persuade; listing routes: Operate inside the same world.
Audience: Gulf agencies evaluating Zenox; simulated Gulf buyers. Job: browse, filter, save, request a viewing. Proof: every control works; bilingual AR/EN with RTL. Constraints: synthetic data labelled as a demo; white/black/beige pinned.
Memorable moment: filling the concierge request slip and receiving a numbered appointment card.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A private agency's concierge stationery, not a listings portal. Refuses the full-bleed villa hero with a floating search bar over a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Bone-white card stock, black letterpress ink, sand/beige paper fields, blind-embossed V monogram, hairline perforations and tear stubs, serial numbers on every card (VE-RYD-0142). Listings are keycard-proportioned cards (1.586:1). Controls are form fields printed on a slip: underlined blanks, stamped checkboxes.

STORY: The visitor understands VELORA handles homes the way a concierge handles a request; believes it through specifics (plot areas, handover dates, serials); acts by filing a request and setting cards aside.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 55%: headline set large in black on white, then the request slip — a sentence with inline blanks (type, city, budget) and a black "Show residences" button. Right: three property keycards fanned on a sand field, the top one live (photo, serial, price). Language switch top-right.

FORM: Hotel concierge stationery kit — position 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 87b75450 (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature interaction: setting a card aside into the Favorites holder; the viewing request issues a numbered appointment card.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
