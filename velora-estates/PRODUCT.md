# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS — pinned by the brief. Deploy target: Vercel (`velora-estates.vercel.app`, not yet deployed).

## Users

Two audiences, in this order:

1. **Prospective freelance clients of Zenox** — Gulf real-estate agencies, brokers and developers evaluating whether Zenox can build their website. They open the live demo from the Zenox portfolio, click through property pages, use filters, save favorites and try Schedule Viewing to judge quality in a few minutes.
2. **The simulated end user inside the demo** — a buyer or tenant in the Gulf browsing luxury homes, comparing properties and requesting a viewing.

## Product Purpose

VELORA ESTATES is a fully working concept website for a luxury Gulf real-estate agency, built as a portfolio piece for Zenox (Web & Mobile Developer). Success: a real-estate client who tries it believes Zenox can ship a complete, polished, bilingual property platform for them.

## Positioning

Not a homepage mockup: every page and control works — search, filters, favorites, property details, agents, locations and a validated viewing request — in Arabic and English with proper RTL.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from the Zenox portfolio (Live Demo button) on desktop and phone. Gulf buyers browse on phones first; agencies review on laptops. Arabic and English are both first-class.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home, Properties, Property Details, Locations, Agents, About, Contact, Favorites.
- Search, filters (location, type, price, bedrooms, status sale/rent), sorting, favorites (persisted in the browser), Schedule Viewing (validated demo form, no backend).
- Market: Gulf — Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Kuwait City and similar; prices in local currency (SAR, AED, KWD).
- Languages: Arabic + English with a language switch and full RTL.
- No backend or accounts; all data is local and synthetic.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VELORA ESTATES.
- Pinned palette from the brief: white / black / beige, luxury character.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. All listings, prices, agents and locations are synthetic demonstration data and must be labelled as a demo (Personal Concept Project by Zenox). Never present them as a real agency, real listings, real people or real clients. Imagery is AI-generated with Higgsfield and must be credited as such.

## Product Principles

1. Every visible control works; nothing is a dead mockup.
2. Honest demo: synthetic data is labelled, never passed off as real.
3. Arabic and English are equal citizens, not a translation afterthought.
4. Phone-first browsing, laptop-grade detail.
